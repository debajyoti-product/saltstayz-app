FAQ

What are the check-in and check-out timings?
Standard check-in time is 2:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is subject to availability and may incur additional charges. Book direct for priority 12 PM check-in.
What is the cancellation policy?
You can cancel for free up to 24 hours before your check-in date. Cancellations made within 24 hours of check-in will incur a one-night retention charge.
Are unmarried couples allowed?
Yes, unmarried couples are welcome. Guests must be 18 years or older and present a valid government-approved photo ID (Passport, Driving License, Voter ID, or Aadhar Card) at check-in. Local IDs are accepted.
Are pets allowed at the property?
Currently, pets are not allowed at this property to ensure the comfort of all our guests.
