About

SaltStayz Premier Naraina puts you at the center of Delhi with 36 well-appointed rooms, a rooftop terrace, and a restaurant that takes food seriously.

5 Room Types, One Address

Standard, Deluxe, Superior, Twin, and Family - five types covering solo travelers, couples, colleagues, and families. The Family Room delivers the space groups need. Twin works with colleagues on a shared stay. Deluxe and Superior bring upgraded comfort for guests who want more from their nights.

Standard: A practical and comfortable base layout designed naturally for solo travelers.

Deluxe: Brings upgraded comfort and premium finishes for guests who want more from their nights.

Superior: An elevated room choice delivering extra comfort and a step up in your stay experience.

Twin: A smart, shared layout built perfectly for colleagues or friends on a trip together.

Family: Delivers the generous extra space and layout configuration that groups and families need.

Only Hotel in Naraina Vihar, Delhi, with a Rooftop Worth Coming Back To

After a long day in the city, the open terrace is where the evening belongs to unwind, catch up with friends, and take in the Delhi skyline. The in-house restaurant serves both vegetarian and non-vegetarian menus prepared separately in dedicated kitchen setups. For guests with dietary preferences or religious requirements. Naraina sits in the thick of Central-West Delhi with direct access to major business districts, markets, and transit hubs. Rajouri Garden, Karol Bagh, and the industrial belt are all within reach.
